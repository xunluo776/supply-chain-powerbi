# Supply Chain & Logistics Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview  
This project analyzes supply chain and logistics performance using Power BI dashboards. It focuses on operational efficiency, delivery performance, and profitability across suppliers, carriers, and sales platforms.

---

## Dashboards  

### 1. Overview Dashboard  
Provides a high-level summary of business performance, including:  
- Total Revenue, Profit, and Profit Margin  
- Total Shipments and Delivered Quantity  
- Inventory levels and stock distribution  
- Supplier lead time comparison  
- Revenue by sales platform  
- Carrier performance based on delayed shipments  

This dashboard helps identify top-performing suppliers, key revenue channels, and operational bottlenecks.

---

### 2. Logistics & Delivery Performance Dashboard  
Focuses on shipment delays and logistics risks, including:  
- Average lead time vs planned lead time  
- Overall delay rate  
- Delay breakdown by route type and transportation mode  
- Impact of disruptions (weather, geopolitical risk, port congestion)  
- External factors such as inflation and risk indices  

This dashboard helps understand where delays occur and what external factors affect delivery performance.

---

## Key Insights  
- Delay rates vary significantly by route and transportation mode  
- Certain carriers contribute more to shipment delays  
- External factors (weather, geopolitical risk) strongly impact delivery reliability  
- Revenue is concentrated among a few major sales platforms  
- Supplier lead times are relatively consistent but still impact efficiency  

---

## Tools Used  
- Power BI  
- Data modeling and visualization  
- KPI-based supply chain analysis
